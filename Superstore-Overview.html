Data notes

The dashboard uses the publicly available Tableau Superstore sample for 2014–2017. The company and its transactions are fictitious.

Sales and profit preserve four-decimal source precision. Margin is total profit divided by total sales. Orders and buyers count distinct IDs on the selected transaction lines. Geography comes from each order’s destination.

Order and ship dates have been corrected for an earlier day/month reversal. Repeated source lines are retained. A product variant preserves its original ID and recorded product name.
